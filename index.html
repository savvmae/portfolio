ABOUT ME

Hey there, I'm a Staff Engineer and tech lead on a UI platform team. I spend my days on design systems, component libraries, monorepo tooling, and the in-house AI tooling my team builds to make everybody else faster. Ten or so product teams build on what we ship. I am well versed in React and TypeScript, but I still have a hard time saying no to a little vanilla JS. For me, the hardest part about development is deciding what to learn next.

I am originally from Charleston, SC, currently residing in Denver, CO. I'll never turn down a good adventure, domestic or abroad. I tackle everything in life head on and without hesitation. I am a straight-shooting gal who's not afraid to roll up her sleeves and solve problems. I'm the mom of three four-legged fur children, one of whom is a shaved cat. I'm an expert at growing okra in the summer, but don't ask me how to organically keep the aphids at bay. I am a self-diagnosed completionist and love when life (code included) comes full circle. I possess a solution oriented mentality and am apt to find problems that need solving before they even arise; it's both a blessing and a curse. I drink more coffee than water in any given day and I have no intention of putting down the coffee cup anytime soon.

Outside of work you'll usually find me above treeline. I chase big days in the Colorado high country, the kind with double digit miles and a few thousand feet of vert, and I keep a spreadsheet of peaks I still owe myself. When I'm not on a summit I'm probably on a lake with a paddle, or watching an unreasonable amount of hockey, which is how Sticktap ended up existing.
